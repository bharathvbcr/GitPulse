The GitPulse code is under the [MIT License](LICENSE). The MIT License covers
the code. It does not cover the name "GitPulse" or the GitPulse logos (the
Pulse G mark and the app icon), which are trademarks of Bharath Chandra
Vaddaram. This page says how you may use them.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your work uses, works with or is based on GitPulse, for example
  "a plugin for GitPulse" or "forked from GitPulse".
- Refer to GitPulse by name in articles, talks, reviews, tutorials and
  comparisons, and show unmodified screenshots of the app.
- Keep the name and logo in an unmodified copy of GitPulse that you build from
  this repository and give away, as long as you do not say or imply that it is
  an official release.

## What needs written permission

- Releasing a modified version, or a fork, under the GitPulse name or logo.
  A fork must use a different name and icon, and may say "based on GitPulse".
- Using the name or logo in your own product name, company name or domain
  name, or in a way that suggests the project endorses or is part of what you
  offer.
- Selling, hosting or supporting GitPulse, or a service built on the GitPulse
  relay, under the GitPulse name.
- Changing the logo, or using it in a way that changes its colours, shape or
  proportions.

## Other projects with their own terms

Vendored code keeps its own licence and copyright notices, listed in
[NOTICE](NOTICE). The GitPulse relay server is a separate project with its own
licence.

## Asking

To ask for permission or report misuse, open an issue in this repository.
When this policy does not answer a question, ask before you rely on it.
